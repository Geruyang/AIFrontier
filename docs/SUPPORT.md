# AI Frontier Support / 支持

Support channel / 支持渠道：https://github.com/Geruyang/AIFrontier/issues

## English

AI Frontier helps you learn AI through English and Simplified Chinese courses, quizzes and public-source news.

- **Change language:** Library > Settings > App language.
- **Restore Pro:** Open the Pro page or Library > Settings > Restore purchases using the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
- **Manage or cancel a subscription:** https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions . Removing the app does not cancel a subscription.
- **News is not updating:** Check your network and refresh Discover. Individual publishers may be temporarily unavailable. iOS decides when background refresh runs.
- **Translation is incomplete:** Check that Apple's language download is complete, then retry from the translation status. The English original is available in article details. Only the title and feed summary are translated.
- **Learning data:** Settings > Delete local learning data removes progress, scores and review history. Saved news remains. There is no app account or developer-operated cloud sync.
- **Report a problem:** Include the app version, device model, iOS version, the screen involved and steps to reproduce. Do not send passwords, payment card details or Apple verification codes.

Contact: https://github.com/Geruyang/AIFrontier/issues. Issues are public; do not post personal or sensitive information. [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

## 简体中文

AI Frontier 提供中英文 AI 课程、测验和公开来源资讯。

- **切换语言：** 资料库 > 设置 > 应用语言。
- **恢复 Pro：** 在 Pro 页面或资料库 > 设置中选择“恢复购买”，使用最初购买时的 Apple 账户。
- **管理或取消订阅：** https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions 。删除应用不会取消订阅。
- **资讯未更新：** 检查网络并刷新资讯页。部分来源可能暂时不可用，后台刷新时机由 iOS 决定。
- **译文未完成：** 确认 Apple 语言资源已下载，再从翻译状态处重试。文章详情可查看英文原文；翻译范围为标题与订阅源摘要。
- **学习数据：** 设置中的“删除本地学习数据”移除进度、分数与复习记录，保留新闻收藏。应用没有账号或开发者运营的云同步服务。
- **反馈问题：** 请描述应用版本、设备型号、iOS 版本、出现问题的页面及复现步骤。不要发送密码、银行卡信息或 Apple 验证码。

联系方式：https://github.com/Geruyang/AIFrontier/issues。问题记录公开可见，请勿发布个人或敏感信息。[隐私政策](PRIVACY.md)。
