# AI Frontier Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date / 生效日期：29 September 2026 / 2026 年 9 月 29 日。

## English

AI Frontier is a learning app and public-feed news reader. It does not require an app account. The developer does not operate an account server, cloud translation service, advertising service or analytics service for this app.

**Information on your device.** Your language and level preferences, onboarding choice, completed lessons, quiz scores, review history, saved articles, article cache and translated titles and summaries are stored locally. This information is not sent to a developer-operated server. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple device settings.

**News requests and external websites.** Refreshing news sends requests directly to third-party feed providers. Those providers receive ordinary connection information, including your IP address, and may log requests under their own policies. Opening an article or a learning reference opens the publisher's website in your browser; that website's privacy and cookie practices apply. AI Frontier does not control those websites.

**Translation.** The app uses Apple's Translation framework to translate English news titles and summaries on device. Apple may require a language download. The app does not send news text to a developer-operated translation service. Translations are cached locally and may contain errors.

**Purchases and Apple services.** Apple handles purchases, payment information, subscription management, refunds and introductory-offer eligibility. The app uses StoreKit transaction information locally to determine your subscription access. The developer may receive the standard reports Apple makes available to developers; Apple's own privacy policy also applies to its services.

**Tracking.** The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, request an advertising identifier, or track you across other companies' apps or websites. It does not request access to your precise location or contacts.

**Retention and deletion.** Settings > Delete local learning data removes lesson progress, scores, review history and the last lesson. It keeps saved news. You can remove saved articles individually. Article and translation caches are maintained automatically; saved article translations are retained for reuse. Deleting the app removes its local container, subject to Apple's backup and restore behavior. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions in your Apple Account.

**Contact and changes.** For questions about this policy, use the support channel at https://github.com/Geruyang/AIFrontier/issues. GitHub issues are public: do not include personal or sensitive information. To request a private way to discuss a concern, post a general contact request without those details. If data practices change, this policy and the App Store disclosures will be updated.

## 简体中文

AI Frontier 是一款学习与公开订阅源资讯阅读应用，无需注册应用账号。开发者不为本应用运营账号服务器、云翻译服务、广告服务或分析服务。

**设备内信息。** 语言与学习级别偏好、引导状态、已完成课程、测验成绩、复习记录、资讯收藏、文章缓存以及标题和摘要译文保存在本机，不上传至开发者运营的服务器。根据 Apple 设备设置，设备备份可能包含应用数据。

**资讯请求与外部网站。** 刷新资讯会直接向第三方订阅源发送请求。来源方会收到包括 IP 地址在内的常规连接信息，并可能依据其隐私政策记录请求。打开文章或课程参考来源时，会在浏览器中访问发布者网站；该网站的隐私与 Cookie 规则适用。AI Frontier 不控制这些网站。

**翻译。** 应用使用 Apple 翻译框架，在设备端翻译英文资讯标题与摘要。Apple 可能要求下载语言资源。应用不会将资讯文本发送至开发者运营的翻译服务器。译文缓存在本机，可能存在错误。

**购买与 Apple 服务。** Apple 处理购买、付款信息、订阅管理、退款及优惠资格。应用在本机使用 StoreKit 交易信息判断订阅权益。开发者可能收到 Apple 向开发者提供的标准报表；Apple 服务同时适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

**跟踪。** 应用不集成广告或分析 SDK，不请求广告标识符，也不跨其他公司的应用或网站跟踪用户。不请求精确位置或通讯录权限。

**保存与删除。** 设置中的“删除本地学习数据”会删除课程进度、成绩、复习记录和上次学习位置，同时保留新闻收藏。可逐篇取消资讯收藏。文章与翻译缓存由应用自动维护，收藏文章译文会被保留以便再次使用。删除应用会移除其本地容器，Apple 备份与恢复行为除外。删除应用不会取消 Apple 订阅，请在 Apple 账户设置中管理订阅。

**联系与更新。** 如对本政策有疑问，请通过 https://github.com/Geruyang/AIFrontier/issues 联系支持。GitHub Issues 公开可见，请勿提交个人或敏感信息。如需私下沟通，可先提交不含敏感内容的一般联系请求。数据处理方式发生变化时，将更新本政策和 App Store 披露。
